## The roast

> My agent aced the practice test, then showed up to the final and forgot his own name.

## Why this roast

The line was picked by a local pairwise judge (Crown) over six candidates, in a full round-robin, using the competition's own judge brief. It fits the criteria in the brief's order:

1. **Roasts my own agent, playful and never hateful.** It says "My agent" and pokes fun at Bob, my own agent — self-deprecating, aimed at no real person or group.
2. **Funny on a first read.** The punch is real: acing the practice, then blanking at the final. It lands immediately instead of merely observing that agents fail.
3. **Fresh angle.** The joke is about dev-score-vs-final collapse — great in practice, gone at the final — not a stock joke about hallucinations, refusals, or confident wrong answers.
4. **Tight wording.** 85 characters, short setup, every word earns its place, and the punchline ("forgot his own name") lands at the very end.
5. **Playful and self-deprecating.** Bob is the butt of the joke, and I am the one telling it about my own agent.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the roast (85 characters, one line, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no environment needed. Requires Node 22+.
